Reading Text

Read Carefully

Ahmed is a young backpacker who loves traveling. Last weekend, he went to Paris, the capital city. He traveled by a fast coach. When he arrived, he saw the big bus station. It was very busy with many people. He felt very happy to be there.

Ahmed’s grandmother lived in Paris many years ago. She told Ahmed that life used to be very different. In the past, people didn’t use to have mobile phones. They used to talk to a tour guide to learn about the city. Grandmother also said that tourists used to carry very heavy luggage because they didn’t have small bags. Ahmed thinks about these stories while he walks.

Today, life is very fast. Ahmed uses his phone to find his accommodation. He walks on the pavement safely. He stops to look at a beautiful fountain near a big statue. The water in the fountain is very clean. Ahmed sees many cars and buses. He waits at the traffic lights when they are red. When the lights turn green, he crosses the street.

Ahmed likes the city today because it is easy to travel. He takes many photos of the clock tower. He sends the photos to his grandmother. Grandmother is happy to see the city again. Ahmed thinks that the city is beautiful now, but he also likes the old stories. It is interesting to see how the city changes. Ahmed wants to visit more cities in the future.
