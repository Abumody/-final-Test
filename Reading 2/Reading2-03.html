My trip to the capital!

From: ali99@email.com | To: salim_travel@email.com

1

Hi Salim,

I hope you are doing well! I am finally visiting the capital city. It is the busiest place I have ever seen, with a huge population. I arrived at the railway station yesterday with my luggage. Since I am traveling as a backpacker, I chose cheap accommodation in the old town instead of a hotel.

2

This morning, I visited the tourist information centre. The tour guide was very friendly and gave me a great introduction to the city's past. I spent the whole morning sightseeing! I saw a tall clock tower and a beautiful fountain in the main square. I also took photos of a statue and a monument. These historical buildings are awesome!

3

Right now, I am sitting in a quiet garden to relax. The temperature is warm and the weather is dry with no rain. It is much nicer than the noisy shopping mall! Getting around is easy, but there is always a traffic jam at the crossroads near the bank. I usually just walk on the pavement and watch the traffic lights. Tomorrow, I will go to the market to buy some gifts for my family.

4

I will remember this experience forever! See you soon.

Best, Ali

Quick Facts:

  • • Learned history from tour guide
  • • Weather: warm and dry

  • • Traffic jam at crossroads near bank
