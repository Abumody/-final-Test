The Magical Library

Read Carefully

Long ago, there was a girl named Lily who loved to explore old buildings. One afternoon, she was walking in a garden when she found a very old, secret door hidden behind some green leaves. She pushed the door open and stepped inside. The hallway was very dark, and she could not see her feet. Lily felt a little bit afraid, but she was also very curious. She walked slowly until she found a large, empty room with high ceilings.

In the middle of the room, she saw a huge wooden table. On the table, there were many different types of books. Lily looked at the titles. She saw a book of poetry and a book about a brave detective. She realized that she had found a magical library. She sat down on the floor and started to read a story about a dragon. Lily was very happy because she loved books more than anything else. She spent the whole afternoon reading in her new favorite place.

Reading Tips:

  • ✓ Notice the main character's name
  • ✓ Pay attention to what she found and where
  • ✓ Look for time words (one afternoon)
  • ✓ Think about why she felt happy
