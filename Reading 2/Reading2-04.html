My wonderful trip to London

An email about a summer adventure

1

Dear Salim,

I am writing this e-mail to tell you about my wonderful trip. Last summer, I went on a special trip to London, which is a very famous capital city. I was very excited because it was my first time traveling alone to visit my cousin. I travelled by a fast train, and when I arrived, I walked out into a huge and busy railway station. There were hundreds of people walking fast, carrying bags and looking at the big screens for their train times. I was only staying for two days, so I did not bring a lot of clothes. I only had one small backpack, which meant I didn't have much luggage to carry around. This made it very easy for me to walk and explore the streets without getting tired. I felt like a real explorer!

2

While I was walking near a big park, I saw something beautiful. In the middle of a garden, there was a giant fountain with water jumping high into the air. The water sparkled under the sun, and I took a beautiful photo to show my mother.

3

Nearby, I saw a group of people listening to a man wearing a uniform. The man was a tour guide who was telling interesting stories about the history of the old buildings. I stood quietly and listened for a few minutes. I learned that some of the buildings were hundreds of years old! After the short lesson, I finally met my cousin, and we went to eat a delicious lunch together. It was a perfect start to my holiday.

4

Yours,
Ahmed
