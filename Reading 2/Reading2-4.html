Email from Khalid

Read Carefully

Hi Sami,

I hope you are having a great week. I am writing to tell you about my wonderful camping trip last weekend. My family and I drove from Muscat to the Green Mountain (Jabal Akhdar). The weather was much cooler there than in the city, and the air felt very fresh.

We arrived on Friday afternoon and set up our large blue tent near some olive trees. My brother and I helped my father find wood to start a campfire. It was hard work, but we had a lot of fun. For dinner, we cooked delicious meat and vegetables over the fire. Everything tastes better when you eat outside under the stars!

On Saturday morning, we went for a long hike. We saw many beautiful birds and even some mountain goats. I took a lot of photos with my new camera to show you. We also visited a small village and bought some local honey and rose water. I really enjoyed the trip because it was very quiet and peaceful. There were no cars or loud noises, just the sound of the wind. I felt very happy and relaxed. Next time, I hope you can join us! It would be even more exciting to explore the mountains together.

Best wishes,
Khalid

  • ✓ Look for who helped with the firewood
  • ✓ Pay attention to the animals they saw
