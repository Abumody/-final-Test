Email from Hamdan to Abdullah

A story about Faisal's visit to Egypt

1

Dear Abdullah,

Thank you for your email. We should be careful when we visit other countries. I want to tell you about my friend Faisal's visit to Egypt with his family. Faisal and his family flew to Cairo for a week. They went to a hotel by taxi. The hotel was in the old city. The old city was noisy, but the hotel was cool and quiet.

2

Faisal and Sarah drank some tea. Then, they stayed in their room and rested. But their son, Nasser wanted to go out.

3

He left the hotel. He walked along the street. He looked at the shops and the old buildings. He turned right. Then, he turned left. He walked along the street. Then, he stopped. He was lost.

4

He saw three men next to a shop. He asked about the hotel. They took him back to the hotel again. He thanked them. They told him that it is better not to go out alone in new places.

5

Please reply as soon as possible. I am eagerly waiting for your email.

Best wishes,

Hamdan
