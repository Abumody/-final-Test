A Day at the Safari Park

Salim's family adventure

1

On Friday, Salim and his family went to the new safari park outside the city. They got up early and left home at seven o'clock. Salim's father bought bus tickets online the night before, so they did not wait in a long line. At the entrance, a friendly guide met them and gave them a map.

2

First, they took a bus around the park. They saw lions, giraffes and elephants. The guide told them not to open the windows. Salim took many photos with his new camera. His little sister was excited when a tall giraffe walked next to the bus.

3

After that, the family went to the picnic area. They ate sandwiches and drank orange juice under a big tree. Then, they walked to the small science museum inside the park. They learned about animal homes and food. Salim enjoyed pressing the buttons and watching short videos.

4

In the afternoon, they watched a short show with animal trainers. A clever parrot said "Hello!" and everyone laughed. At four o'clock, they were tired but happy. On the bus back home, Salim looked at his photos and smiled. He decided to write about the day in his diary.
